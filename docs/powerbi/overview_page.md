# Overview Dashboard

## Objective

This page provides a high-level view of IT operations.

It helps answer:

- How many tickets are handled?
- Is SLA performance acceptable?
- Are there trends over time?

---

## KPIs

- Total Tickets  
- SLA Compliance Rate  
- Assignment Breach Rate  
- Action Breach Rate  

---

## Visuals

### Tickets Over Time

Shows ticket volume trend.

Business question:
Is demand increasing or decreasing?

---

### SLA Compliance Trend

Displays SLA compliance over time.

Business question:
Is SLA performance stable?

---

### Tickets by Priority

Breakdown of tickets by priority level.

Helps identify:
- High urgency workload
- Operational pressure

---

### Tickets by Category

Shows distribution of issues.

Helps understand:
- Main problem areas
- Where improvements are needed

---

## Key Insights

- Ticket volume can change over time
- SLA performance may vary by period
- Some priorities dominate workload
- Certain categories generate more tickets

---

## Notes

- SLA compliance is based on assignment and action conditions
- Trends are calculated using the date dimension
