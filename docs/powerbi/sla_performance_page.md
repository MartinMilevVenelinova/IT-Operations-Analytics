# SLA Performance Dashboard

## Objective

This page analyzes SLA performance in detail.

It helps answer:

- Where does SLA fail?
- Is the problem in assignment or resolution?
- Which areas have more breaches?

---

## KPIs

- SLA Compliance Rate  
- Assignment Breach Rate  
- Action Breach Rate  
- Median Resolution Time  

---

## Visuals

### SLA Trend by Type

Shows SLA performance over time.

Includes:
- SLA compliance
- Assignment breaches
- Action breaches

Business question:
Is SLA performance improving or worsening?

---

### SLA by Category and Priority (Heatmap)

Matrix visual showing SLA performance.

Helps identify:
- Problem categories
- High-risk priorities

---

### Assignment vs Action Breaches

Compares the two types of SLA failures.

Business question:
Where is the main issue in the process?

---

### Resolution Time Distribution

Shows how long tickets take to resolve.

Helps detect:
- Delays
- Inefficiencies

---

## Key Insights

- SLA breaches can happen at different stages
- Assignment delays may impact overall SLA
- Some categories have lower SLA compliance
- Resolution time is a key factor

---

## Notes

- SLA compliance requires both assignment and action conditions
- Median is used instead of average to reduce outlier impact
