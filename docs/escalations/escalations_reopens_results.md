# Escalations and Reopens Results

## Objective
Summarize the main SQL results for escalation and reopen analysis.

## KPI 1 - Escalation Rate
- Total tickets: 70,985
- Escalated tickets: 19,064
- Escalation rate: 26.86%

## KPI 2 - Reopen Rate
- Reopened tickets: 3,342
- Reopen rate: 4.71%

## KPI 3 - Wrong Escalation Rate
- Wrong escalations: 1,852
- Wrong escalation rate: 9.71%

## KPI 4 - Escalation Return Rate
- Returned escalations: 3,995
- Escalation return rate: 20.96%

## Main Findings
- Around 27% of tickets are escalated.
- Reopen rate is low at 4.71%, indicating relatively stable resolutions.
- Around 10% of escalations are incorrect.
- Around 21% of escalations are returned, showing process inefficiencies.

## Key Insight
Not all returned escalations are wrong, but a significant portion of escalations generates rework between support groups.
